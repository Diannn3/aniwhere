## Overall assessment

The references establish a recognizable product identity: cream reading surfaces, olive actions, soil-brown accents, generous serif titles, agricultural imagery, and a map as the main discovery tool. The strongest patterns are the desktop search/map composition in 2, the mobile map/sheet in 6, and the aligned desktop comparison in 8. Preserve those. The set is useful art direction, but its displayed evidence and calculations cannot be copied as real product data.

The major problems are unsupported match percentages, invented buyer verification and metrics, inconsistent location/price/capacity data, unexplained cost deductions, and phone layouts that shrink too much content into one viewport. Generic dashboard features also expand beyond the one-week scope. A raster mockup can demonstrate hierarchy and legibility, but cannot prove keyboard access, responsive behavior, actual color tokens, routing, or business truth.

## Shared design and content contract

- One UI per output image. No contact sheet, montage, split desktop/mobile presentation, second device, or art-board title outside the UI.
- Mobile outputs are direct portrait app-screen captures with no physical phone bezel; desktop outputs are direct landscape browser-content captures with no laptop mockup. This preserves space for readable controls.
- Supply the corresponding original UI and image 11 as actual image inputs on every built-in image-generation call. Each agent must inspect both with view_image first.
- Preserve warm editorial identity: Source Serif 4-like display headings and Source Sans 3-like body text, restrained agricultural photography, controlled whitespace, subtle separators, small consistent outline icons.
- Palette: Field Olive #597928; Young Leaf #91AC67; Soil Brown #6E3511; Rice Cream #FCECD8; Warm Surface #FFFDF8; Field Ink #20251E; Route Blue #4E7380. Natural photography may contain other colors. Interface surfaces/actions use the supplied palette.
- Essential text is Ink on Warm Surface or Cream. Primary actions use Warm Surface text on Olive. Avoid small Leaf-colored text and small Olive or Blue text on Cream. Rendered pixels require later implementation contrast testing.
- No repeated handwritten slogans, kicker headlines, gratuitous status dots, forest-green gradients, emoji-style icons, medals, arbitrary match scores or invented success metrics.
- English UI with an EN / FIL switch. The original Tagalog headlines remain useful later localization references; one selected language governs a screen.
- Farmer UI remains accountless; a buyer workspace can be authenticated. Do not introduce checkout, payment, reservations, automatic dispatch, chat inbox, AI recommendations or harvest surveillance through a mockup.
- A visible, unobtrusive notice reads "Demo — sample data" on any screen showing prices, quantities, buyer records or business results. This is a meaningful product state, not an internal generation label.
- Geographic content reads "Illustrative map". Draw a quiet stylized map; no invented travel times, road distances, navigation accuracy or claim of live tiles.
- Match terms: "Matches your harvest", "Accepts part of your harvest", "Contact to confirm", "Does not match". Smaller layouts may use "Full match" / "Partial match" only with an immediate crop/quantity explanation. Matching never guarantees purchase.
- Source/validity for fixtures: "Sample offer · 17 Sep 2026"; no automatic "updated today". Include "Confirm terms before travel" near contact where practical.
