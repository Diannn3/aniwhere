01

Mobile harvest entry

A compact path from crop, quantity and location to discovery.

Audit focus: remove unsupported match counts before searching, use numeric quantity input, simplify navigation and give the search action enough space.

02

Desktop homepage

The map and harvest form lead the page.

Audit focus: one search flow, Laguna scope, useful introductory copy and no fabricated adoption statistics.

03

Mobile buyer workspace

A manageable list of offers and their review status.

Audit focus: published, in-review and draft offers; replace unsupported inquiry statistics and a map of private farmers with a clear management task.

04

Mobile place detail

Terms, harvest fit and the cost calculation in one place.

Audit focus: replace the undefined 94% score and unsupported verification badges; explain PHP 8,400 gross less PHP 600 entered transport, leaving PHP 7,800.

05

Mobile comparison

Accepted quantity and entered costs remain visible together.

Audit focus: show actual transport charges, preserve the 100 kg remaining at the partial option, and remove unsupported best-option claims.

06

Mobile discovery

A quieter map supports a readable results sheet.

Audit focus: descriptive fit states, explicit Map/List control, consistent sample prices and no invented precise road distances.

07

Desktop place detail

The same terms as the mobile detail, with more room to inspect them.

Audit focus: consistent price and accepted quantity across devices; remove fabricated private contact details, reviews and registration claims.

08

Desktop comparison

Three aligned options with the deductions made explicit.

Audit focus: gross minus entered transport equals PHP 7,800 / PHP 9,300 / PHP 5,700. The third option accepts only 200 kg. Equal visual treatment avoids an unexplained recommendation.

09

Desktop buyer workspace

Review and maintain buying offers without a distracting analytics dashboard.

Audit focus: counts derived from the three visible records, explicit publication states and guidance for reviewing current terms.

10

Desktop discovery

Filters, results and map share the same harvest context.

Audit focus: consistent filters and results, a compact harvest summary, explicit fit explanations and no mixed-crop average-price metric.
